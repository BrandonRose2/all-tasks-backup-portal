Hi JR,

I hope you're doing well. I am reaching out to collect some key financial figures for the properties in your region. Please complete the attached Excel spreadsheet and return it to me at your earliest convenience.

  1. Current Rents — the monthly rent currently being charged per unit type (Studio, 1BR, 2BR, 3BR, 4BR, 5BR)
