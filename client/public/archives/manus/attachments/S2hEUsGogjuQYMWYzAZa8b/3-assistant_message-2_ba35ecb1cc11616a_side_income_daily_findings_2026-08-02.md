## Cross-cutting cautions

1. Treat flexible-platform rates as gross receipts, not take-home pay; vehicle, supplies, platform fees, and taxes can materially change net income.
2. Never pay a third party for access to jobs, training, checks, or “activation” outside the official platform’s disclosed process. Use only official URLs above.
3. Keep a simple hours/revenue/expense log for the first two weeks, then continue only with paths that produce a strong effective hourly rate after costs.
4. Do not use copyrighted, trademarked, plagiarized, or unlicensed content in Etsy/POD/KDP products or AI-assisted service work.

## Suggested treatment of earnings in the final report

Use the following labels consistently: **official posted rate** (DataAnnotation, Outlier); **official commission/royalty** (Amazon Associates, KDP); **marketplace rate reference** (Upwork VA/bookkeeping); **self-set starter price / unit-economics scenario** (Etsy, print-on-demand, tutoring, local gigs); or **variable per-task payout** (Clickworker, Instacart, DoorDash, Rover, Taskrabbit). Avoid averaged income claims when source evidence is unavailable.

## Suggested immediate prioritization logic

1. For near-term cashflow, choose one offline/local platform (DoorDash, Instacart, Rover, Taskrabbit) plus one low-barrier remote platform (Clickworker or generalist AI work), based on transport, location, and eligibility.
2. For stronger professional income, create a narrowly focused Upwork/Fiverr/LinkedIn Service offering and Wyzant tutor profile, then apply to DataAnnotation for STEM/math/accounting expertise.
3. Treat Etsy, Printify, KDP, and Amazon Associates as catalog/content businesses with uncertain and delayed sales; create a small original product batch only after cashflow options are live.
4. Do not attempt all platforms at once. Use 30–60 minute setup blocks and a daily application/offer target.
