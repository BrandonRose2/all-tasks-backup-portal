Safety and decision rules

Protect time, identity, and cash while testing opportunities.

Never pay for the promise of a job. The FTC advises job seekers to research the company, speak with someone trusted, never deposit a check and send money back, and never pay a recruiter or employer for access to work. Use only official platform workflows and URLs.
  • Track gross income, platform fees, mileage, supplies, hours, and payout dates from day one.
  • Do not use copyrighted, trademarked, plagiarized, or unlicensed content in digital products, print-on-demand listings, or AI-assisted services.
  • Use direct, written scopes for freelance projects: deliverable, revision count, price, deadline, and what the client must provide.
  • Judge local gig routes by net return after vehicle and time costs, not by the app’s displayed gross offer.
