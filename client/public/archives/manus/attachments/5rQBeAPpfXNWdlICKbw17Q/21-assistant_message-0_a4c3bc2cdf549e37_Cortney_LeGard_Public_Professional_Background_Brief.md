## Dinner Questions That Keep the Conversation Professional

You do not need to ask for detailed financial documents at dinner. A calm, businesslike discussion can focus on the intended transaction path and let both sides decide whether to move forward under a mutual confidentiality agreement.

| Topic | Neutral question to ask |
|---|---|
| Buyer identity | “Which entity would be the buyer, and who would have final authority to sign a letter of intent?” |
| Interest and rationale | “What specifically about our company fits your operating plans and long-term strategy?” |
| Funding process | “If we both want to continue after initial discussions, what is your normal process for demonstrating financing capability or proof of funds?” |
| Team and continuity | “How would you think about our employees, equipment, customers, and family transition support after a closing?” |
| Diligence sequence | “Would you be comfortable beginning with a mutual confidentiality agreement, then exchanging a defined set of information before discussing detailed valuation?” |
| Legal/process readiness | “Who are the attorney and accounting professionals you would expect to involve if the conversation advances?” |

## Practical Bottom Line
