## Report treatment rules

- Use **up to** maximum rates and per-sale royalty structures as explicit platform-stated figures, never as expected hourly or monthly income.
- For delivery/gig work, show a gross-income range only when independently estimated; prominently flag fuel, vehicle depreciation, unpaid waiting time, insurance, and taxes.
- For marketplace and passive-income paths, prioritize transparent time-to-first-revenue descriptions over overly precise income estimates.
- Present Toptal, AI-specialist roles, bookkeeping, and advanced tutoring as specialist options rather than entry-level quick wins.
