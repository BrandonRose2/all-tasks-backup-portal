## Executive decision: prioritize active income first

For immediate household cash flow, the strongest routes are **specialist tutoring, technical/AI services, piano instruction, local task work, and pet care**. Digital products and affiliate marketing can be worthwhile long-term, but they are **not** reliable emergency-income sources; assume little or no revenue during their first 30–90 days.

## 7. Fraud, privacy, and profitability checks

Never pay for a job, “equipment reimbursement,” training access, account activation, or a check-cashing request. The only specific onboarding charge confirmed in this report is TaskRabbit’s $25 fee in applicable cities; use only its official registration path. [10] Amazon Associates is free to join and explicitly warns that it has not authorized businesses to sell paid setup or consulting services. [20]

Keep marketplace communications, contracts, and payments on-platform. For delivery work, log mileage and calculate net hourly earnings after fuel, maintenance, insurance, and unpaid waiting time. For AI work, never paste client confidential information into a model without written client permission and an approved data-handling method. For digital products, sell only original work or properly licensed material; do not use copyrighted sheet music, brand logos, or protected designs.

> **Practical rule:** if an offer promises unusually high pay for unskilled work, asks for money before work begins, pushes communication off-platform, or refuses to state the deliverable/rate in writing, decline it.
