## Full Job Description

**Job Overview:** We are seeking a highly organized and proactive Personal Assistant to support our CEO in a fast-paced environment. The ideal candidate will possess exceptional clerical skills and a strong ability to manage multiple tasks efficiently. This role requires a professional demeanor, excellent phone etiquette, and the ability to maintain confidentiality while handling sensitive information. This job involves a fair amount of driving the CEO and his family for errands, school, lessons, etc.

---

## Responsibilities

- Manage the CEO's Outlook Calendar, scheduling appointments and coordinating meetings.
- Provide clerical support including data entry, filing, and proofreading documents.
- Assist in office management tasks to ensure smooth daily operations.
- Handle phone calls with professionalism, addressing inquiries and relaying messages as necessary.
- Organize and plan events, ensuring all details are executed flawlessly.
- Maintain an organized filing system for both physical and digital documents.
- Prepare reports and presentations as required by the CEO.
- Act as a liaison between the CEO and other team members or external contacts.

---

## Qualifications

- Proven experience as a Personal Assistant or in a similar administrative role.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite, particularly Outlook Calendar.
- Strong organizational skills with the ability to prioritize tasks effectively.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
- Attention to detail with strong proofreading abilities.
- Experience in event planning is highly desirable.
- Ability to maintain confidentiality and exercise discretion at all times.
- A proactive approach to problem-solving with strong decision-making capabilities.
